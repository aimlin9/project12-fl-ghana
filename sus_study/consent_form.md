# Informed Consent — Dashboard Usability Study

**Project:** Cross-School Federated Learning for Privacy-Preserving Student Progress
Tracking (Project 12, Group 7)

## What you're being asked to do

You're being invited to try out an admin dashboard for about 10–15 minutes and
then fill in a short questionnaire (10 quick statements) about how easy or hard
it was to use. There is no right or wrong way to use it — we want your honest
first impression.

## What this is and isn't

- This is a **simulation**. No real student data is shown at any point — every
  number you see is either synthetic/simulated or drawn from a public,
  anonymised research dataset (OULAD). You will never see any real person's
  academic record.
- Your feedback is used **only** to compute a System Usability Scale (SUS)
  score for our mini-project evaluation. It is not shared outside the group
  and supervisor.

## Your rights

- Participation is **entirely voluntary**.
- You may **stop at any time**, for any reason, without needing to explain why
  and without any penalty.
- Your responses are **anonymous** — we do not record your name against your
  answers.
- You are not being deceived about anything in this study; what you're shown
  is exactly what it appears to be — a simulation dashboard.

## Consent

By signing below, you confirm that:
- You are an adult (18+) participating voluntarily.
- You understand this is a simulation and no real student data is involved.
- You understand you may withdraw at any time without penalty.
- You consent to your anonymous SUS questionnaire responses being used in
  this mini-project's evaluation.
